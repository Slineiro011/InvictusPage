# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React (static site, no backend)

## Users

Two audiences: (1) prospective members in Pereira, Colombia who are curious about freediving/apnea and deciding whether to reach out and join a class; (2) the existing community, sponsors, or federation contacts who view the club as an established, credible sports organization. Primary job across both: quickly understand what the club is and where it trains, then decide to make contact.

## Product Purpose

An informational one-page site for Invictus Apnea, a competitive freediving (apnea) sports club based in Pereira, Colombia. It exists to present the club's identity and training program and to convert interested visitors into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = the visitor understands what the club offers and where, and taps through to WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A local, real, competitive freediving club training at a specific municipal facility (Complejo Acuático Villa Olímpica, Pereira) under a named coach — not a generic swim school or a stock diving-tourism brand. The supplied photos are actual members and training sessions, not stock imagery, and that authenticity is the point.

## Operating Context

Training happens at Complejo Acuático Villa Olímpica, Pereira. Instruction is led by coach Jhon Harold Giraldo. Members train in a swimming pool (dynamic/static apnea, fin work) — evidenced by the supplied photos/video of pool sessions, underwater shots, and a group photo at the venue.

## Product Principles

1. Real over generic: every visual and claim traces back to the club's actual people, place, and coach — never stock or generic diving marketing.
2. One clear action: every path through the page leads to a WhatsApp conversation with the club, not a form or account.
3. Don't invent facts: pricing, schedule, and additional credentials are currently absent — design and copy must not fabricate them; the WhatsApp CTA exists to cover that gap.
4. Water is the aesthetic: blue dominates as the emotional register (depth, calm, focus of apnea), with the logo's red used sparingly as an accent, never competing with blue for dominance.
